A Tribute to A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India

A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, the 11th President of India and a pioneer of the country's space and missile programmes.

Timeline of Kalam’s Life

  • 1931: Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  • 1954: Graduated in Physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli.
  • 1960: Degree in Aeronautical Engineering from Madras Institute of Technology.
  • 1969: Transferred to ISRO from DRDO.
  • 1980: India’s first indigenous SLV-III successfully launched Rohini Satellite.
  • 1980s-90s: Developed Agni and Prithvi Missiles.
  • 1992-1999: Chief Scientific Advisor to PM & Secretary of DRDO.
  • 1998: Coordinated Pokhran-II nuclear tests.
  • 1999-2001: Principal Scientific Advisor to Government of India.
  • 2002-2007: Served as the President of India.
  • 2015: Passed away on July 27 during a lecture at IIM Shillong.

"Dr. Kalam uniquely combined the honesty of a child with the energy of a teenager and the maturity of an adult."
