📖 About Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam

Known as the Missile Man of India.

Served as the 11th President of India (2002–2007).

Played a key role in India’s Pokhran-II nuclear tests in 1998.

An inspiration to millions through his vision, humility, and dedication.
